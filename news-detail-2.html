Mubadala participates as strategic shareholder in Loscam alongside Trustar Capital, FountainVest and Sinotrans

(Beijing/Hong Kong, October 22) Mubadala Investment Company, Trustar Capital, FountainVest, and Sinotrans Limited jointly announced today the completion of the equity transfer transaction regarding Loscam International Holdings Limited.

Following the completion of the transaction, Mubadala Capital and Trustar will each hold 30%, while FountainVest and Sinotrans will each hold 20%. This transaction marks Mubadala's first joint buyout investment in the Asian industrial sector.

As a leading provider of returnable logistics packaging pooling services in the Asia-Pacific region, Loscam was founded in 1942 and is headquartered in Hong Kong.

The global pallet market is showing strong growth, with pallet pooling playing a crucial role in helping enterprises reduce costs, increase efficiency, and enhance supply chain security.

Li Shiji, Vice General Manager and Board Secretary of Sinotrans, stated: 'The addition of Mubadala marks a new stage of internationalization.'>

Mohamed Albadr, Head of Asia at Mubadala, stated: 'We are very pleased to partner with Trustar Capital, FountainVest, and Sinotrans.'

Zouhir Regragui, Head of Industrial and Business Services Investments at Mubadala, added: 'Pallet pooling is a key component of modern logistics.'

Wang Ranxu, Partner at Trustar Capital, stated: 'Mubadala's investment is an important milestone in Loscam's development journey.'

FountainVest Managing Directors stated: 'We warmly welcome Mubadala's investment in Loscam.'

Andrew Law, President of Loscam, stated: 'Mubadala's investment opens an exciting and landmark new chapter for the Loscam team.'
