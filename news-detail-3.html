CITIC announces additional capital injection to accelerate McDonald's China into a new stage of development

On October 21, CITIC Limited announced that its private equity investment arm, Trustar Capital, alongside CITIC Corporation, has reached a supplementary agreement to acquire shares in its East Asia operations in Chinese Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macao.

CITIC Capital stated: 'McDonald's China is a landmark investment project we are proud of. Since our investment in 2017, it has maintained a steady development outlook.'

Zhang Yichen, Chairman of CITIC Capital and Chairman of the Board of East Asia China, stated: 'As the controlling shareholder of East Asia China, we will spare no effort to drive China into a new stage of development.'
