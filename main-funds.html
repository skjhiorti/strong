Trustar Capital Partners

Our buyout strategy is the cornerstone of our 22 years of success. Leveraging deep local expertise and a broad global network, we have generated exceptional returns by acquiring and transforming businesses that capture China's growth and productivity enhancement opportunities. Our proven value-creation approach makes Trustar Capital a trusted partner for corporations and management teams seeking to maximize operational potential in China's dynamic market.

Trustar's China buyout investment team comprises over 30 professionals with diverse backgrounds and deep expertise. The team is organized into 5 dedicated sector-focused sub-groups, supported by over 30 operating partners. We focus on carve-outs, roll-ups, take-private transactions, and corporate buyouts, leveraging extensive industry resources, global vision, and deep professional knowledge to help portfolio companies unlock and realize their value.
