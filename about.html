Overview

Trustar Capital is a leading private equity firm in Asia with an established buyout platform in China. With offices in China, Japan and the United States, we invest globally in companies that demonstrate the potential to fully leverage China's economic growth and productivity enhancement, backed by extensive experience.

We take pride in our unique investment platform, characterized by our unparalleled China resources, rigorous investment strategy, and professional staff. With unique insights, we help companies discover value-creation opportunities, supported by a comprehensive buyout ecosystem and a diverse professional team with extensive functional experience. We enable portfolio companies to maximize their potential through market expansion and operational optimization.

Since 2002, we have completed over 100 investments and currently manage committed capital of $10.5 billion.
